FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does dzz cost?

Nothing. A launch costs pump.fun's account rent, about 0.0055 SOL paired with SOL (0.007 with HYPE, 0.011 with a fee split), plus any dev buy you choose.

How long is too long?

On a modern PC, 5 characters take about a minute exact or seconds in any case, and 6 in any case a minute or two. 7 exact is days. The speed table above is measured on your own device.

Is the coin a normal pump.fun coin?

Yes. dzz uses pump.fun's own program and create instruction. The coin trades on pump.fun's curve and graduates the same way; only its address is your choice.

Where are the keys kept?

In this browser only. Clearing the site's data deletes found addresses you haven't used. A launched coin no longer needs its key.

Which characters can I use?

Solana addresses use base58: no 0, O, I or lowercase l. In any case mode, an l matches L and an o matches o.

Which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare and Backpack in the browser.
