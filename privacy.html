Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

dzz is a tool for launching coins on Solana at a contract address you choose. This page says what dzz keeps about you and why.

What dzz keeps

  • Your account: an id, a display name and a profile picture link.
  • Sign-in accounts you connect: for Google, Discord or X, the account's id, name, handle and picture link, so you can sign in with it again. dzz does not keep your email, your password or any access token from them.
  • Wallets you link: their public addresses, which you prove by signing a message. dzz never holds a private key of your wallet.
  • Coins you launch: the coin's public details (name, ticker, picture, contract address, the launch transaction), which are public on Solana anyway.

What stays on your device

The private keys of the addresses dzz finds for your coins are made and kept in your browser (or in the desktop app on your computer). They are never sent to dzz.

How it is used

Only to sign you in and to show your launches and creator fees in one place. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Removing your data

You can remove sign-in accounts and wallets from your account page at any time. To delete the whole account, contact us on X.

Services dzz uses

Cloudflare (account storage), Vercel (the website), Solana RPC providers and Jupiter (public chain data and prices), and Google, Discord and X when you choose to sign in with them.
